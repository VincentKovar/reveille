What mornings are like

  • A poem, not a beep. Short public-domain poems chosen for mornings: something happens, the images surprise you, and the ending stays open.
  • A voice you choose. With a free Google Gemini key, a natural voice reads to you and a new poem is found for you every day. Without one, your phone's built-in voice reads.
  • It will wake you. The voice fades in gently. If it can't start, or you drift back off, a bell chime climbs to full volume until you're up.
  • A few lines before the day starts. Tap "I'm up" and a small journal asks what the poem left you with.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above. If Chrome warns about the file type, tap Download anyway. It warns about every app that isn't from the Play Store.
  2. Tap Open when the download finishes. If Android says installs from this source aren't allowed, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect doesn't recognise the developer, tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Open Reveille and follow the short setup. It walks you through the voice, your first alarm, and the permissions it needs to ring on the lock screen.
Getting a free Gemini key
  1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key, then Copy.
  3. Paste it into Reveille's setup (or Settings) and tap Check.

It's free for personal use. The key is stored only on your phone and sent only to Google.

An alarm didn't ring
  1. In Reveille, open Settings → Alarm permissions. Every row should have a check mark.
  2. Turn up your phone's alarm volume (Reveille uses the alarm volume, like the Clock app).
  3. On Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Huawei and Oppo phones: long-press the Reveille icon → App info → Battery → Unrestricted.

Add to iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Open Reveille above.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Reveille from its new icon and follow the short setup.
  4. Every night: open Reveille, tap Start nightstand mode, plug in your phone, and turn the volume up.

Why nightstand mode? Apple only lets App Store apps wake a locked iPhone. Reveille isn't in the App Store, so it needs to stay open on screen overnight, like a bedside clock. Full iPhone guide
