About Reveille Rules

• Public domain poems under 20-30 lines.

• Prioritizes narrative scenes over static descriptions.

• Unresolved endings that leave creative space for your morning thoughts.

Known Limitation

This is a static web app with no backend, so it can't guarantee firing while your phone is locked or the app is fully closed in the background — Android will throttle timers in a suspended tab.

For reliable wake-ups: install this to your home screen, keep the app open in the foreground, and leave your phone plugged in overnight (a real "alarm clock on the nightstand" pattern).
